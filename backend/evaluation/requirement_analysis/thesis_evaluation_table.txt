Interpretation Notes

1. The standalone FR/NFR classifier achieved 78.0% accuracy and a Macro F1 score of 0.7799 on the reviewed external evaluation set.

2. The standalone NFR category classifier achieved 44.0% accuracy and a Macro F1 score of 0.3206, indicating weaker performance on fine-grained quality-attribute classification.

3. The end-to-end NFR classification chain achieved 32.0% accuracy because FR/NFR classification errors can prevent the NFR category classifier from being executed.

4. The rule-based ambiguity detector achieved 100% precision but only 38.46% recall, showing that deterministic rules are reliable when they flag ambiguity but miss many contextual ambiguity cases.

5. The rule-based incompleteness detector achieved zero recall because it mainly detects explicit indicators such as TBD and TBA, while the reviewed dataset contains broader contextual forms of incompleteness.

6. The final hybrid FR/NFR classification achieved 80.0% accuracy and a Macro F1 score of 0.7997.

7. Hybrid ASR detection achieved 84.0% accuracy, with precision, recall, and F1 all equal to 0.8621. This was one of the strongest contextual-analysis results.

8. Hybrid ambiguity detection improved over the rule-only approach. Recall increased from 0.3846 to 0.6154 and F1 increased from 0.5556 to 0.7619 while maintaining 100% precision.

9. Hybrid incompleteness detection improved over the deterministic rules, but recall remained low at 0.1000, indicating that contextual incompleteness remains difficult to detect reliably.

10. Hybrid NFR quality-attribute classification achieved 32.0% accuracy and a Macro F1 score of 0.2228. Performance remained weak for minority categories such as Fault Tolerance, Legal, Portability, and Scalability.

11. Conflict detection and potentially-missing detection each achieved 100% accuracy; however, the evaluation dataset contained no positive gold examples for these categories. Therefore, precision, recall, and F1 are not meaningful for these two tasks.

12. The final hybrid evaluation processed all 50 requirements across 5 projects, giving 100% evaluation coverage.

13. Overall, the results support the hybrid architecture combining deterministic rules, fine-tuned RoBERTa classifiers, and Gemini-based contextual reasoning, while also highlighting limitations in fine-grained NFR classification and incompleteness detection.

* No positive gold examples were available for Conflict Detection or Potentially-Missing Detection.
