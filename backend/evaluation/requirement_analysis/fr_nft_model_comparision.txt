Selection Reason
----------------
RoBERTa was selected because it achieved the highest Weighted F1-score
(0.9043) and the highest precision (0.9058) on the held-out FR/NFR test set.

Although DistilBERT achieved the same accuracy (0.9041) and was faster,
RoBERTa provided the best overall classification performance according
to the selected F1-based model-selection criterion.
