==================================================
7. CURRENT INTERPRETATION
==================================================

The evaluation results support the use of a hybrid
requirement-analysis architecture.

The FR/NFR RoBERTa classifier demonstrates useful
generalization on the external human-reviewed set.

The NFR-category classifier is substantially weaker,
particularly for minority quality-attribute classes.

The rule-based ambiguity detector is highly precise
but misses contextual ambiguity.

The rule-based incompleteness detector cannot capture
broader semantic incompleteness.

These results justify combining:

1. deterministic rules,
2. fine-tuned transformer classifiers, and
3. LLM-based contextual reasoning

within the complete Requirement Analysis module.
