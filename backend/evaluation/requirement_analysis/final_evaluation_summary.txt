Final interpretation
--------------------
The final hybrid Requirement Analysis pipeline demonstrated strong
FR/NFR classification and ASR identification on the held-out benchmark.

Fine-grained NFR quality-attribute classification improved substantially
after introducing class-weighted training and targeted domain-generic
augmentation, although minority classes such as Maintainability remain
challenging.

Ambiguity detection achieved high recall but lower precision, indicating
a tendency to over-flag ambiguous requirements.

Conflict and potentially-missing detection require separate evaluation
with positive examples before strong performance claims can be made.

The results should be interpreted as performance on a controlled,
small-scale held-out benchmark rather than evidence of unrestricted
real-world generalization.
